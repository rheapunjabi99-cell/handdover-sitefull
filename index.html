What we do, how we do it, and what your team gets out of it.

What we do

We prepare your support employees to move into customer onboarding roles.

  • One focused move: from customer support to customer onboarding.
  • For Indian SaaS teams where AI has freed up support capacity and onboarding has a backlog.
  • Built around your product, not generic courses.
How we do it

Realistic practice, expert review, then real work under supervision.

  • 3 weeks of simulations (about 4 hours a week): take over a stalled customer rollout built from your workflows.
  • Twists and decision replays: employees see what they missed, then try again.
  • AI feedback in the moment; experienced onboarding leads review the reasoning.
  • A supervised rotation on real onboarding work before any transfer.
The value to you

Fill onboarding roles faster and more safely, with the main fee tied to results.

  • People who already know your product, so you skip months of product training.
  • Decisions on evidence: a readiness report for every employee.
  • Low risk: the main fee is due only after a successful transfer and 30 days in the role.
  • A real next step for your best support people, so they grow with you instead of leaving.

Why now

Ticket volume is down. The onboarding backlog isn’t.

As AI handles more support tickets, many SaaS teams have people they could redeploy, while customer implementations wait for someone to run them. Before opening another hiring requisition, look at who already knows your product.

What your support team already knows

Product knowledge you’d otherwise spend months teaching a new hire.

  • Which configurations trip customers up
  • What needs explaining twice
  • When to pull in engineering

What onboarding asks of them

Different skills that a strong support record doesn’t prove.

  • Understanding what a customer actually needs
  • Planning an implementation and holding the timeline
  • Getting a customer live and using the product

HANDOVER closes that gap and shows you who’s ready, before you make the move.

How it works

Take over a stalled project. Prove it on a new one.

Three weeks of practice built around your own workflows, then supervised real work before anyone decides on a transfer.

Step 1, week 1

Take over a stalled implementation

Handover notes, customer tickets and a half-finished plan, using synthetic customer data.

Step 2, weeks 1–2

Handle the twist

The data turns out unreliable, or the plan exceeds the team’s capacity. They revise.

Step 3, week 2

Replay the decision

See the evidence they missed, take a short lesson on that exact gap, then try again.

Step 4, week 3

Prove it on a new case

A new customer and different data, then a short defence of their choices with a reviewer.

Step 5, rotation

Do supervised real work

Real onboarding tasks with a manager watching, before any formal move.

AI gives instant, specific feedback Experienced onboarding leads review the reasoning Simulated work is always labelled

FAQ

Questions leaders ask us

Straight answers, including when HANDOVER isn’t the right choice.

Do you guarantee that employees will transfer?

No. We don’t promise jobs or promotions. That’s why the main fee is paid only when someone actually moves and meets the standards you agreed after 30 days.

How much of my team’s time does this take?

About four hours a week per employee for three weeks, plus a supervised rotation whose length we agree with you. A manager needs to be available to supervise that real work.

Do you need access to our customer data?

No. We build cases from your workflows and processes using synthetic customer data. Experienced onboarding leads check that the cases reflect how your team actually works.

Why not just promote our best support people?

You can, and often should. HANDOVER helps when it isn’t obvious who’s ready, when informal moves have gone wrong before, or when managers don’t have time to coach each person from scratch.

When is this not the right fit?

If you can’t release people from support without hiring replacements, or onboarding has no open roles, external hiring or ordinary coaching will probably serve you better.
